## Analysis

### SSD Cache Impact

The SSD cache delivers the largest relative speedups on the Flip, where cold prompt evaluation is 6-13x slower than on Strix Halo. On a 15K-token prompt, the Flip's GLM-4.7 goes from 439s cold to 2.68s warm - a 164x improvement that turns an unusable 7-minute wait into a 2.7s startup.

On Strix Halo, the SSD cache still provides 5-43x speedups, but cold starts are already fast enough (1.7-42s) that the cache is a convenience feature rather than a usability requirement.

### Warm TTFT Convergence

Despite the massive hardware gap, warm TTFTs are remarkably close across tiers. Both machines restore from SSD and hit the first token in 0.2-2.7s regardless of model size. The SSD cache effectively normalizes startup latency across hardware.

### Gemma-4 Architecture Advantage

Gemma-4's warm prompt eval on Strix Halo hits 35,400 TPS - 2.8x faster than the next best (Qwen3.6 at 14,189 TPS). This is because Gemma-4 is a standard MoE (no Mamba/attention hybrid), so KV cache restore involves simpler recurrent state validation. On the Flip, the same model hits 21,502 warm TPS - still the fastest, but by a smaller margin.

### MoE Generation Speed

Generation speeds are largely hardware-bound and quant-agnostic on the same tier:

| Tier | Model | Gen ms/tok (cold/warm) |
|------|-------|----------------------|
| halo | gpt-oss-120b Q8 | 28.5 / 27.0 |
| halo | GLM-4.7 Q8 | 46.4 / 34.1 |
| halo | Qwen3.6-35B Q8 | 29.4 / 27.5 |
| halo | gemma-4-26B Q5 | 27.5 / 24.6 |
| handheld | GLM-4.7 Q4 | 161.9 / 153.3 |
| handheld | gemma-4-26B Q5 | 62.2 / 62.6 |
| handheld | Qwen3.6-35B Q4 | 51.3 / 48.3 |

GLM-4.7 is notably slower to generate on Strix Halo (34-46 ms/tok) compared to other models (24-28 ms/tok) despite a similar parameter count. This is consistent with its larger vocabulary (256K vs 152K) and heavier tokenizer.

### Context Scaling

The Strix Halo's 96GB VRAM ceiling enables 131-196K context at Q8_K_XL quants. The Flip's 24GB total GPU memory ceiling requires lower quants (Q4_K_M/Q4_K_XL) and caps context at 32-65K depending on model size. Gemma-4 at Q5_K_M fills the Flip's VRAM at just 32K context due to its larger per-layer state.
